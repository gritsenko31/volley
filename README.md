## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Hit the ball over the net and make it land on your opponent's side. First player to reach the winning score wins the match!

### Controls

**Player 1 (Left Side):**
- **W** - Jump
- **A** - Move left
- **D** - Move right

**Player 2 (Right Side):**
- **Arrow Up** - Jump
- **Arrow Left** - Move left
- **Arrow Right** - Move right

**Mobile:**
- **Left/Right Buttons** - Move player
- **Jump Button** - Jump to hit ball

### Game Rules
- Ball must go over the net
- Ball landing on opponent's ground scores a point
- Hitting the ball gives it upward momentum
- Time your jumps to spike the ball down
- Net touch doesn't count as fault
- First to 15 points wins (or custom score)
